# Project Use

DSH FreeCanvas may be used for canvas creation inside DSH, custom development, model-provider integration, and AI image, video, or text workflows.

MIT-covered components may be used commercially under their existing terms without a separate commercial license. The public DSH plugin may also be used commercially within Elastic License 2.0, but it may not be offered as a hosted or managed service exposing a substantial set of its features, and license-key functionality may not be circumvented. Future Pro or enterprise modules may require a separate commercial agreement or EULA.

See [Licensing](/docs/business/license) before distributing, embedding, or commercializing a specific component.

Use GitHub Issues only for public product feedback and reproducible technical problems. Do not post confidential business or security information.
